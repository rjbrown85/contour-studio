Drawing options

Finger touches only move the canvas in this mode. A mouse still works.

Grid guides never appear in exports. Shape recognition handles roughly straight lines, boxes, and ovals. Uncertain marks stay freehand.

Colour, width, fill, and opacity apply to your selection or your next mark.

A little room to draw

Pencil preserves your hand-drawn line. Eraser removes whole marks as you drag. Locked objects and layers are protected.

Curve draws an arc or S-curve. Select it and drag the two coloured control points to reshape it.

Snap shapes tidies rough lines, boxes, and ovals when you lift. You can also enable “Hold at the end” in •••. Use “Keep original stroke” after recognition to restore the hand-drawn version.

Layers lets you add, reorder, rename, hide, and lock layers. Select any object and use Lock to keep it in place. Unlock it from the same selection controls.

Colours has a colour wheel, HEX entry, separate fill/outline colours, and a saved brand palette. Adjust gives exact dimensions and alignment to the page.

Fit page shows the whole drawing. Fit width enlarges it to the available width. The right-hand scroll rail moves up and down when zoomed in. Grid and snapping are in •••.

Draw makes smooth vector lines. Drag to make a line, box, or oval. Tap with the text tool to add a label.

Select an object to move it. Drag its corner to resize or its round handle to rotate. Back and Front change its stacking order.

Two fingers move and zoom the canvas. Pan also works with one finger. Enable Pencil only in ••• to keep fingers from drawing.

Save project under the drawing name keeps an editable copy. Open that file on your desktop to continue. Export SVG for another vector editor.

No account or cloud sync. This browser stores one current drawing. Save a project file for a durable backup. Safari and Chrome keep separate browser storage.

Keyboard: B draw · P pencil · E eraser · C curve · V select · L line · R box · O oval · T text · H pan · ⌘/Ctrl Z undo · Shift ⌘/Ctrl Z redo · Delete remove · arrows nudge · Escape deselect. Hold Shift to constrain shapes.
