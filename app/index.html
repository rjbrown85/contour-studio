Draw makes smooth vector lines. Drag to make a line, box, or oval. Tap with the text tool to add a label.

Select an object to move it. Drag its corner to resize or its round handle to rotate. Back and Front change its stacking order.

Two fingers move and zoom the canvas. Pan also works with one finger. Enable Pencil only in ••• to keep fingers from drawing.
